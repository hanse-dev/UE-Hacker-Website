# Week 3 – Conditions (if-else)

## Topic: Decisions in code

Learn how your program can make decisions. With if-else you determine which code gets executed.

## The three worlds

### 🗡️ Adventure World
Master the ways of fate and program the future of your hero.

### 🐴 Horses World
Make smart decisions at the stables – which horse to train and when.

### 🚀 Sci-Fi World
As a commander, every order decides life and death on dangerous missions.

## Learning goals

- **if conditions** for simple decisions
- **if-else** for two paths of choice
- **if-elif-else** for multiple decisions
- **Comparison operators** (`==`, `!=`, `<`, `>`, `<=`, `>=`)
- **Logical operators** (`and`, `or`, `not`)
- **Nested conditions** with correct indentation
