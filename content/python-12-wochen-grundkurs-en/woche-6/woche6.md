# Week 6 – Lists

## Topic: Collecting and organising data

Learn how to store and manage multiple pieces of data in a list. Lists are like digital collections that you can extend and search through as you like.

## The three worlds

### 🗡️ Adventure World
In the king's treasure chamber you learn how to collect and organise endless items.

### 🐴 Horses World
In the stables you learn how to perfectly organise training plans and horse records.

### 🚀 Sci-Fi World
On the space station you manage crew members and missions in intelligent lists.

## Learning goals

- **Create lists** with square brackets
- **Add elements** with append() and insert()
- **Remove elements** with remove() and pop()
- **Search lists** with index() and the in-operator
- **Sort lists** with sort() and sorted()
- **Nest lists** for complex data structures
