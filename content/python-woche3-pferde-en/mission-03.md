# ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ Mission 3: The Tournament

At the tournament, riders are rated by their performance! The score is calculated like this:

`score = points - (errors * 5) - (time / 10)`

- **Gold:** score >= 85 **and** no errors
- **Silver:** score >= 70 **and** at most 2 errors
- **Bonus:** anyone faster than 60 seconds gets extra points

**Bonus (optional, not checked):** Also take the difficulty of the test into account!
