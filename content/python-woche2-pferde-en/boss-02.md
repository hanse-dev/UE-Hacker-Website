# ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Extra Challenge 2: The Training Hall

> ⚠️ Harder! The extra challenges combine everything from this week – they are optional.

In the training hall you analyse five exercises with duration (whole minutes) and intensity (decimal number from 1 to 5): describe them with f-strings, sum up duration and intensity, and mark demanding exercises with a boolean.

**Bonus (optional, not checked):** Calculate the efficiency (intensity / duration × 10) for each exercise!
