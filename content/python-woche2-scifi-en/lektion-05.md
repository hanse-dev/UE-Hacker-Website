# 📟 System Protocol 5: Booleans

A **Boolean** (`bool`) has only two possible values: `True` or `False` – like a switch that is on or off. Perfect for system states.

```python
system_online = True
alarm_active = False
print(type(system_online))   # <class 'bool'>
```

> ⚠️ **Important:** Write `True` and `False` with a capital first letter and **without** quotation marks – `"True"` in quotes would just be text!

Booleans also come out of comparisons: `80 > 50` gives `True`.
