# ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ Extra challenge 1: The Feed Room

> ⚠️ Harder! The extra challenges combine everything from this week – they are optional.

The feed room holds four sacks. Every one must be catalogued: feed type (text) and weight (decimal number). Then you calculate the total and average weight and check whether sack 2 is heavier than the average.

**Bonus (optional, not checked):** Count how many sacks are below the average weight!
