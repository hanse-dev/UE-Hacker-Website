# ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ Mission 3: The Time Paradox

You are stuck in a time paradox and are jumping through the years – only clever loops can help you escape.

**Step 1** – Jump back in time until you reach the starting year.
**Step 2** – Limit the number of jumps.
**Step 3** – Print status reports.

**Bonus (optional, not checked):** Change the jump size and see how the number of jumps changes.
