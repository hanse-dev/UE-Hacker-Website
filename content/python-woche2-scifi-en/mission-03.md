# ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ Mission 3: The Asteroid Mining

Calculate the yield from asteroid mining! Two asteroids are available:

| Asteroid | Mineral content (kg) | Purity (0–1) | Value per kg |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ceres-9 | 500 | 0.8 | 12.5 |
| Vesta-3 | 300 | 0.9 | 20.0 |

The **raw value** is: mineral content × purity × value per kg.

**Bonus (optional, not checked):** Calculate how much more valuable the better asteroid is!
