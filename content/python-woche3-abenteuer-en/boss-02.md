# ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Extra Challenge 2: The Dungeon Simulator

> ⚠️ Harder! The extra challenges combine everything from this week – they are optional.

The simulator has **5 levels** with increasing requirements. Each level uses a different kind of decision: `and`, `if-else`, `if-elif-else`, nested conditions and a mixture of `or` and `and`.

Hint for level 5: use **parentheses** to group conditions, e.g. `potions >= 3 or (runes and escape_route)`.

**Bonus (optional, not checked):** Add time limits for each level!
