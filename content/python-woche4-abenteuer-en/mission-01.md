# ⭐⭐☆☆☆ Mission 1: The Number Wizard

In the Tower of Repetitions you practise the power of numbers and shape magical number patterns.

**Bonus (optional, not checked):** For every number from 1 to 10 print both the number and its square (1×1, 2×2, 3×3, …) and format the output so it looks like a magical tablet.
