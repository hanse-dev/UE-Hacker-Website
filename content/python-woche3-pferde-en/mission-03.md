# ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ Mission 3: The Tournament

At the tournament, riders are rated by their performance. The **total score** is calculated like this: `score = points - (errors * 5) - (time / 10)`. For **gold** you need a score of 85 or more **and** no errors, for **silver** 70 or more and at most 2 errors. Anyone under 60 seconds gets a time bonus.

**Bonus (optional, not checked):** Also take the difficulty of the test into account!
