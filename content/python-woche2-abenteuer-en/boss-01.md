# ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ Extra Challenge 1: The Treasure Room

> ⚠️ Harder! The extra challenges combine everything from this week – they are optional.

The Tower Guardian opens the final door: the treasure room has **8 chests**, and each one needs to be catalogued! The chests hold 120, 80, 200, 150, 90, 300, 60 and 100 gold (in this order).

Hint: `>` compares two numbers – the result is a boolean (`True` or `False`), e.g. `9 > 5` gives `True`.

**Bonus (optional, not checked):** Count and print how many chests hold less than the average.
