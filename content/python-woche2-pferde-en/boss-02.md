# ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Extra challenge 2: The Training Hall

> ⚠️ Harder! The extra challenges combine everything from this week – they are optional.

In the training hall you analyse the exercise plans. Each exercise has a name (text), an intensity (decimal number), a duration in minutes (whole number) and a difficulty from 1 to 5 (whole number). Print descriptions, add up the durations, calculate the average intensity and mark demanding exercises with a `bool`.

**Bonus (optional, not checked):** Calculate the efficiency (intensity / duration × 10) for each exercise!
