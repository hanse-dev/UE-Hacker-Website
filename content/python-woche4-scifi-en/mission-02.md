# ⭐⭐⭐☆☆ Mission 2: The Space Station Commander

You are responsible for all 10 decks of the space station and must keep track of alarms, bonuses and maintenance.

**Step 1** – Program the deck scanner.
**Step 2** – Manage warnings and bonuses.
**Step 3** – Maintenance and mission end.

**Bonus (optional, not checked):** Add your own message for each deck, for example a different one for the upper and the lower decks.
