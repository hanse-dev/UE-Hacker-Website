# ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ Extra Challenge 1: The Tower of Knowledge

> ⚠️ Harder! The extra challenges combine everything from this week – they are optional.

The Tower of Knowledge must be documented for future heroes! The old tower holds a poem about the power of programming. Write it down: create four variables `line1` to `line4` with one sentence each, print everything neatly and sign as the author.

**Bonus (optional, not checked):** Add a comment with a hidden message.
