# Week 4 – Loops (for, while)

## Topic: Repeating code

Learn how to repeat code automatically. Instead of writing the same code a thousand times, tell Python: "Do that automatically for me!"

## The three worlds

### 🗡️ Adventure World
In the magical tower you discover how patterns repeat and cycles shape reality.

### 🐴 Horses World
On the training ground you learn the rhythm of riding – repetition leads to perfection.

### 🚀 Sci-Fi World
In the Chronos Tower you manipulate time itself with time-loops and cycles.

## Learning goals

- **for loops** with `range()` for controlled repetitions
- **while loops** for flexible conditions
- **Loops over strings and lists**
- **break and continue** for loop control
- **Nested loops** for complex patterns
- Recognise and avoid **infinite loops**
