# ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ Extra challenge 1: The Prime Number Decryption

⚠️ **Harder!** This challenge combines everything from this week.

A **prime number** can only be made by multiplying 1 and itself. To test a number `n` without division, try all pairs `a * b` with `a` and `b` from 2 up to `n - 1` (two nested loops). If any pair gives `n`, then `n` is **not** prime.

**Bonus (optional, not checked):** Extend the range to 2 – 50 and find the largest prime.
