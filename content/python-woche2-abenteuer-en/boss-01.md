# ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ Extra Challenge 1: The Treasure Room

> ⚠️ Harder! The extra challenges combine everything from this week – they are optional.

The treasure room has 8 chests, each with a gold value. Catalogue them: calculate the total value and the average, and compare one chest against it. The chests are already given in the variables `chest1` to `chest8`.

Hint: `>` compares two numbers – the result is a boolean (`True` or `False`), e.g. `9 > 5` gives `True`.

**Bonus (optional, not checked):** Find out which chests are above the average.
