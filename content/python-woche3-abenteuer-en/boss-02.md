# ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Extra Challenge 2: The Dungeon Simulator

> ⚠️ Harder! The extra challenges combine everything from this week – they are optional.

The simulator has 5 levels with rising demands, plus a final score! Every level needs a different kind of condition – from a simple `if` to `and`/`or` and nested checks.

Hint for level 5: `and` binds more strongly than `or`. With **parentheses** `( )` you decide yourself what is evaluated first.

**Bonus (optional, not checked):** Add time limits for each level!
