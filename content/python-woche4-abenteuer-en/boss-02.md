# ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Extra Challenge 2: The Magic World

> ⚠️ Harder! The extra challenges combine everything from this week – they are optional.

Create a small magic world as an **8×8 grid** in which a wizard (`M`) moves towards a magical goal (`Z`). Empty squares are dots (`.`).

**Bonus (optional, not checked):** With every move, print the wizard's coordinate as a square (e.g. "Wizard on square C3").
