# ⭐⭐☆☆☆ Mission 1: The Quantum Counter

Master quantum time in the Chronos Tower and train different counting patterns with for loops.

**Step 1** – Generate timestamps from 1 to 20.
**Step 2** – Count special patterns: even numbers and a countdown.
**Step 3** – Perform a time analysis: the sum of all numbers.

**Bonus (optional, not checked):** Create a squares table: for each number from 1 to 10 print the number and its square (`n * n`).
