# ⭐⭐☆☆☆ Mission 1: The Four Elemental Stones

At the entrance of the tower lie four pedestals – one for each element. Fill them with your elemental stones: 🔥 Fire (text), 🪨 Earth (whole number), 💧 Water (decimal number) and 💨 Air (truth value).

**Bonus (optional, not checked):** Use `type()` to confirm the element of all four stones.
