# 🎯 Part 1: Discovering and creating objects!

You have lots of data and functions. Instead of mixing everything up, you can bundle them into logical units.

With classes and objects your code becomes neat, clear, and reusable!

## 🎮 Theme worlds to choose from

- **🗺️ Adventure World:** The craft guilds of Pyralia – create your first magical class!
- **🐴 Horse Stables:** The structure of the stables – organise horses and equipment as objects!
- **🚀 Sci-Fi World:** The foundations of the space station – build your first ship classes!

# 🎯 Learning goals (Part 1)
- What are classes and objects?
- Create simple classes
- Understand and use attributes
- Define and call methods
- Instantiate objects
