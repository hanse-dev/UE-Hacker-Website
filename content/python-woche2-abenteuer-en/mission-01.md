# ⭐⭐☆☆☆ Mission 1: The Four Elemental Stones

The Tower Guardian opens the first door of the Elemental Tower: *"Three trials. Whoever passes them masters all four elements."*

At the entrance lie four pedestals – one for each element. Fill them with your own elemental stones and print each with an f-string.

**Bonus (optional, not checked):** Use `type()` to confirm the element of all four stones.
