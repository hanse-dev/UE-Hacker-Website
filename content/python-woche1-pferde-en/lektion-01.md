## 🐎 What is a lesson?

A **lesson** is like a training session for you and your horse – a fixed exercise that you learn and master together. Horses need clear instructions, and so do computers! In Python these instructions are called **functions**.

You **call** a function by writing its name and putting parentheses after it. What you hand to it goes inside the parentheses:

```python
print("Hello")   # function: print – what we hand over: "Hello"
```

> 🐴 **Remember:** lesson = function. The **name** says what should happen, the **parentheses `()`** call it. Without parentheses nothing happens! Later, in Week 5, you will even invent your own functions.

## 📣 Lesson 1: Call

`print()` is your first command – a **function** that you call. It displays text on the screen.

**Important:** text must always be in quotation marks `"` or `'`! An empty `print()` prints an empty line – handy for a better overview.
