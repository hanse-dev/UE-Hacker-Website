# ⭐⭐☆☆☆ Mission 1: The Bridge Guardian

A troll-like guardian blocks the bridge! Convince him to let you pass. The rule of the bridge: you may cross if you are **at least 18 years old**, **or** you have **at least 10 gold**, **or** you own a **pass**.

**Bonus (optional, not checked):** Only charge a toll if the hero has no pass!
