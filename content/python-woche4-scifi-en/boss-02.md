# ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Extra challenge 2: The Holodeck

⚠️ **Harder!** This challenge combines everything from this week.

Create a virtual holodeck with a chessboard grid and a player who moves towards a target. Tip for the chessboard: use a variable `dark = True` and flip it with `dark = not dark` after every field – and once more at the end of each row.

**Bonus (optional, not checked):** Make the grid bigger (10 × 10) or let the player start in another corner.
