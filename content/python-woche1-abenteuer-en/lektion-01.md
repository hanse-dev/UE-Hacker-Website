## 🧙 What is a spell formula?

A **spell formula** is like a magic incantation: a fixed sequence of words that has a particular effect. In programming these are the **commands** of Python – and there they are called **functions**.

You **call** a function by writing its name followed by parentheses. Whatever you hand to the spell (the ingredient) goes inside the parentheses:

```python
print("Hello")   # spell: print – ingredient: "Hello"
```

> 🪄 **Remember:** spell formula = function. The **name** says which spell, the **parentheses `()`** cast it. Without parentheses nothing happens!

Every formula you master makes you a mightier wizard! Later, in Week 5, you will even learn to invent **your own** spell formulas (functions).

## ✨ Spell Formula 1: Incantation

`print()` is your first magic command – a **function** you call. It displays text on the screen.

```python
print("Hello, world!")
print("Welcome to Pyralia!")
```

**Important:** text must always be in quotation marks `"` or `'`! An empty `print()` prints an empty line – handy for readability.
