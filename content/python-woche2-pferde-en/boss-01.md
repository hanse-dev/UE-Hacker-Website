# ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ Extra Challenge 1: The Feed Room

> ⚠️ Harder! The extra challenges combine everything from this week – they are optional.

The feed room has 8 sacks. Each one needs to be catalogued! You write a protocol with f-strings, calculate the total weight and average, and compare with a boolean.

**Bonus (optional, not checked):** Count and print how many sacks are below the average!
