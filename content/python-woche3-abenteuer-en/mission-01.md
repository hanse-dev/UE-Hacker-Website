# ⭐⭐☆☆☆ Mission 1: The Bridge Guardian

A troll-like guardian blocks the bridge! Convince him to let you pass. He lets through everyone who is **at least 18 years old OR has at least 10 gold OR owns a pass** – use the `or` operator for this.

**Bonus (optional, not checked):** Only demand a toll if the hero has no pass!
