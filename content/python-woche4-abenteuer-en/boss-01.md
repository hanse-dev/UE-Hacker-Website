# ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ Extra Challenge 1: The Magic Number

> ⚠️ Harder! The extra challenges combine everything from this week – they are optional.

Magic numbers are hidden in the tower: the **multiples of 7** between 7 and 100. Decipher them, count them and make them visible.

**Bonus (optional, not checked):** Extend the range to 1000 and see how the count and sum of the magic numbers change.
